Why this build: in v0.1.36 a real "lay" arriving mid-pipeline waited
74 s — runCommand awaited the drain of ALL in-flight demo turns
(up to 4 planned turns x ~20 s each). Trang: one turn is 20 s
(walk ~5 + bow 3 + draw 2 + wait 10) — a real guest should wait at
most ONE turn, not four.

New in v0.1.37 (all in index.html; sw.js only cache/version):
  - NAME-SWAP QUEUE: when a real "lay" arrives while demo turns are
    in flight, the guest takes over the NEAREST in-flight demo
    performer (still walking in, nearest to the one who is bowing —
    usually a few metres away, already en route). The performer
    takes the real guest's name (tag redrawn); the seated real-guest
    actor takes the performer's fake name and stays put. The renamed
    performer walks the remaining distance and performs the REAL
    3-bow sequence, then waits for xin xam / ve cho as usual. The
    other in-flight performers KEEP GOING (no recall) and queue
    behind the real guest; the bower is never swapped and always
    finishes. Each further real guest takes the next-nearest
    still-unclaimed performer (B, then C, then D). If no performer
    is claimable (only the bower is in flight), the guest waits for
    the next rise (bounded 25 s) or walks from their own seat.
    Real raLay now resolves in ~one turn (target bow start <= 20 s)
    instead of ~74 s.
    Consequence: the #61 listener's 65 s timeout is plenty again —
    NO listener timeout raise is needed.
  - GATE-RACE FIX (Cuu verified v0.1.36 FAIL, comment 7060160965):
    3 demo performers stood on the bow point AT ONCE for ~40 s, 2
    bowing at the same time. Root cause: the stage gate granted entry
    by resolving the waiter's promise, but demoStageHolder was only
    set after the final 1.05 m step (~1 s later), so a second advance
    in that window admitted the next performer too. Fixed: the stage
    is now CLAIMED synchronously at grant time (demoStageMaybeAdvance
    sets demoStageHolder before resolving), and the stage-end clears
    are guarded to what the turn still holds. The race was latent in
    v0.1.36's gate; the swap queue (two claimed turns near the stage
    at once) is what made it reachable. Self-test: 0 double-stage in
    6 probe attempts, max 1 performer ON the bow point (within
    0.3 m), max 1 bowing at a time.
  - GATE PRIORITY for claimed turns: a swapped-in real guest jumps
    the gate queue and waits only for the current bower to finish —
    never for the demo turns queued behind them. Without this,
    correct serialization made the first guest wait behind every
    queued demo turn (50 s measured, 146 s worst case). Among claimed
    waiters, FIFO (guest 2 waits behind guest 1's full turn: 56 s
    measured — by design, still inside the 65 s listener timeout).
  - NEAREST SEAT FIRST: the planner prefers the NEAREST eligible seat
    (shortest demo path to the bow point) instead of cycling through
    the seats in order, so most turns are ~20 s and the pipeline
    carries fewer long walks. Fairness: a seat is not picked again
    within the last 6 picks; far seats still get turns, just less
    often, always with the distance-aware early departure (A8/A9).
    Walk speed stays 1.0 m/s, never faster.
  - Nothing else changes in normal operation (no real guest waiting):
    15 s cadence, ~15 s arrival gaps, rising wave, fortune persists
    2 min decoupled, carpet 2.4 m, cushions 0.40 m x 0.01 m, no demo
    label, thienNhom pause, ambient 30-45 s, real-command priority.
